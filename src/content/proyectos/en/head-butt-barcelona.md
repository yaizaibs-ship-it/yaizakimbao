## The challenge

Build a vintage football shirt brand from scratch that didn’t look like just another shirt shop. It had to speak football, fashion and internet culture, and work on a swing tag, on Instagram and in a shop in Barcelona’s Gothic Quarter.

## Strategic approach

The concept comes from a single second: on 9 July 2006, in Berlin, Zidane ended his career with a headbutt in the World Cup final. Painted, sculpted, memed and worn: one second of football turned into culture.

HEADBUTT starts there: a Barcelona archive of authenticated vintage shirts for people who read football as style and memory. *“Football is a language of identity. The shirt is its alphabet.”*

## Visual solution

- **Logo:** custom heavy italic condensed lettering. The headbutt illustration splits HEAD and BUTT exactly at the point of contact, inside a green field that works like a flag.
- **Colour:** grass green (#298E3A) leads, night blue anchors and red is only a signal, below 2%.
- **Typography:** Sztos for the logo, Bebas Neue for headlines and prices, Space Grotesk for text and DM Mono for catalogue data.
- **Shop:** a locker room with astroturf walls, a goal net, pitch markings and shirts hung by name and number.
- **Applications:** shopping bag, a hang tag with club, era, number and condition, and stickers.

It all comes together in a brand book, a custom Shopify store, the logo animation and launch content.
