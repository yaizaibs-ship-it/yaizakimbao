## The challenge

Build a vintage football shirt brand from scratch that didn’t look like just another shirt shop. It had to speak football, fashion and internet culture, and work on a swing tag, on Instagram and in a shop in Barcelona’s Gothic Quarter.

## Approach

A Barcelona archive of authenticated vintage shirts, told as culture rather than as a catalogue. *“Football is a language of identity. The shirt is its alphabet.”* It all comes together in a brand book with usage rules, a custom Shopify store, the logo animation and the launch content.
