## The challenge

KIMBAO 999 is a creative direction project that explores identity as an evolving process, connected to fashion, the body and visual culture. The challenge was to build a brand that isn’t fixed: a flexible system that adapts, changes and grows over time.

## Strategic approach

Creative direction is used here to give coherence to ideas, visuals and narratives across very different formats, moving between digital and physical spaces. Design isn’t just an aesthetic tool but a language to express transformation and continuity.

From the need to transform clothes so they truly accompany your life came **Workshop 999**: workshops that teach people how to do it, giving garments a new life and creating a style that feels personal and conscious. A space to experiment, learn and have fun, using fashion as a way to express identity and creativity in everyday life.

## Visual solution

A visual identity system developed from concept to application, including art direction, styling and brand thinking. The logo, in pink, white and black, sits alongside raw, everyday photography (supermarkets, the street, the worktable itself) and pieces such as the bag made from a recycled Doritos packet and a kids’ Barça shirt.

The project was also presented at a fair with a physical installation that followed the same philosophy as the garment transformation: making the most of the resources available. The chairs were recycled, the tablecloths were made from my grandmother’s fabric scraps and the small tables were built from fruit crates. Everything aligned with the brand’s values.

The result is a cohesive yet mutable universe, built to evolve without losing its core.
