## The challenge

Turn waste and forgotten garments into one-off pieces, with a language of their own somewhere between pop culture, football and craft.

## Strategic approach

An imagined “collab” between FC Barcelona and Doritos: two consumer icons reworked by hand. Upcycling as a creative statement, not just a sustainable one.

## Visual solution

A one-off bag sewn by hand from a recycled Doritos packet and a kids’ Barça shirt, in orange with a blaugrana gradient.
