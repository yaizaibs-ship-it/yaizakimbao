## The challenge

KIMBAO 999 is a creative direction project that explores identity as an evolving process, connected to fashion, the body and visual culture. The challenge was to build a brand that isn’t fixed: a flexible system that adapts, changes and grows over time.

## Approach

Creative direction is used here to give coherence to ideas, visuals and narratives across very different formats, moving between digital and physical spaces. Design isn’t just an aesthetic tool but a language to express transformation and continuity.
