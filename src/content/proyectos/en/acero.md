## The challenge

Design the identity for ACEROº, an energy drink that wanted to step away from the traditionally masculine, aggressive look of the category and stand out with a distinctive aesthetic of its own.

## Strategic approach

Instead of lightning bolts, black and chrome, the brand leans on transparency: natural ingredients —stevia, strawberry juice, vitamins— presented as if in a lab. The honesty of the product becomes the visual language.

## Visual solution

A white can where the ingredient list is the typographic hero, a lab-style label system with batch numbers, dates and barcodes, and a vivid red as the accent. The identity is completed with the brand’s website.
