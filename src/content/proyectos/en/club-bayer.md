## The challenge

Design newsletters for Club Bayer that would work across different markets and lift user activation and engagement.

## Strategic approach

Each send was adapted in design and content to its region and tested with A/B variants. Click and engagement analysis, together with the marketing team, fed back into the strategy for the next send.

## Visual solution

A modular newsletter system —training content, themed campaigns, key dates such as International Women’s Day— with a clear hierarchy and prominent calls to action.
