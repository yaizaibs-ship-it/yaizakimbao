## The challenge

Make a portfolio that is itself a sample of what I do: identity, art direction and motion. And one I could look after myself, without depending on anyone every time I finish a project.

## Approach

A few carefully chosen pieces: one typeface, one accent colour and a scroll rhythm that tells each case in acts. The site is bilingual (Spanish and English), fast, and publishes itself every time I change something.

## Solution

- **Design:** off-white turning to black, a single type family (Geist) and magenta #e5007e as a signature.
- **Animation:** flowers that grow to full screen, pinned sections, a horizontal carousel and details that only appear on hover.
- **Content:** each project is a text file with gallery, videos and mosaic blocks, in two languages.
- **Tech:** Astro, GSAP with ScrollTrigger, and Lenis for smooth scrolling. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
